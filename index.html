Science explained

Reality has better plot twists than fiction.

From a clock on Earth to a clock near a massive object, physics tells us that time is not experienced identically everywhere. From memory to distant galaxies, modern science keeps replacing simple assumptions with deeper ideas.

Cosmora separates established scientific evidence from hypotheses and speculation. Articles are written to explain ideas clearly without pretending that unanswered questions have easy answers.
About Cosmora

A home for curious minds.

Cosmora is an independent science and curiosity publication concept focused on accessible explanations. Our goal is simple: take fascinating questions, explain what scientists actually know, and make the journey enjoyable.

For publication, individual articles should be reviewed against authoritative scientific sources and updated when evidence changes.
