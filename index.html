FAQ

Good to Know

Why does a code say expired?

Redeem codes can have limited validity or usage limits. An expired code cannot be activated.

Are the codes guaranteed to work?

No. Codes are subject to Garena's availability, region, expiry and usage rules.

Do I need to click ads to get a code?

No. Codes are not conditional on ad clicks. Advertising should remain separate from access to codes.
